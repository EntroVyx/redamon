# RedAmon Trademark Policy

RedAmon's source code is free and open source under the [MIT License](LICENSE).
That license covers the code only. It does not give anyone rights to the
RedAmon name or logo. This policy explains how you may use them.

## The marks

This policy covers:

- the name **RedAmon™**, in any capitalisation or spelling (REDAMON, redamon,
  Red Amon);
- the **RedAmon logo**, the red Eye of Horus shown in
  [docs/assets/logo.png](docs/assets/logo.png), on its own or together with the
  name.

Both belong to Samuele Giampieri and are called "the marks" below.

## Why this policy exists

People choose a security tool because they trust it. Anyone who runs software
called RedAmon should be able to rely on getting the official project, with its
code and its security fixes, and not a modified copy they have no way to check.
This policy leaves the code free to use, change and share, and keeps the name
tied to the official project.

## Uses that need no permission

- **Referring to the project.** You may say that your product "works with
  RedAmon", "integrates with RedAmon", "is based on RedAmon" or "is a fork of
  RedAmon", as long as the statement is true.
- **Writing and talking about it.** Articles, blog posts, reviews, tutorials,
  videos, talks, courses, academic papers and security reports may use the name,
  and may show the unmodified logo to illustrate the project.
- **Using it in your work.** You may say that you use RedAmon in your
  assessments, research or services.
- **Redistributing official releases.** Unmodified copies of official RedAmon
  releases may keep the name and logo.
- **Plugins and integrations.** You may name your work "*Your Name* for
  RedAmon", as long as your own name comes first and is the prominent part, and
  nothing suggests the project made or endorses it.
- **Community groups.** Non-commercial meetups, user groups and chat communities
  may use the name, as long as they say they are community-run and unofficial.

## Uses that need written permission

- Naming a product, service, company, fork, Docker image, package, domain or
  social media account "RedAmon", or anything confusingly similar, such as
  "RedAmon Pro", "RedAmonX", "Red-Amon" or "RedAmon Cloud".
- Distributing a modified version under the RedAmon name or with the logo.
- Offering a hosted or managed service named after RedAmon.
- Using the logo, a modified version of it or a design derived from it as part
  of your own branding.
- Selling merchandise with the name or logo.
- Suggesting that the project endorses, sponsors, certifies or partners with
  you or your product.

## Never allowed

- Using the marks in connection with illegal activity, including testing systems
  without authorization (see [DISCLAIMER.md](DISCLAIMER.md)).
- Using the marks for malware, or in any way that misleads people or harms the
  project's reputation.

## If you fork RedAmon

The MIT License lets you fork RedAmon. If you **distribute** your fork to
others:

1. Give it a new name that is not confusingly similar to RedAmon.
2. Remove the RedAmon logo and use your own.
3. You may say in your README and description that it is "based on RedAmon" or
   "a fork of RedAmon", with a link to this repository.
4. Keep the MIT copyright notice, as the license requires.

You do not need to rename a fork that you only use to develop and send pull
requests to this repository, or a modified copy that you run only inside your
own organisation and do not present to others as official RedAmon.

## Attribution

When you use the marks in a published work, please add this line where it
fits:

> RedAmon and the RedAmon logo are trademarks of Samuele Giampieri.

## Permission and reporting misuse

To ask for permission, or to report a use of the marks that breaks this policy,
write to **samuele@redamon.org** and describe the use.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated. The version in this repository is the current one.
Nothing in it limits uses that the law already allows, such as fair use or
truthful references to the project.
